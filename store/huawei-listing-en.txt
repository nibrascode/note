App name: Nibras Note
Category: Tools / Productivity (Notes)
Short description (80):
Paper notebooks on your phone — lined, grid, dotted. Private and offline.

Full description:
Nibras Note turns your phone into a set of paper notebooks.

• Notebooks with covers on a shelf, sortable by date, shape or name
• Paper styles: lined, narrow / wide ruled, grid, dotted, blank, Cornell, list, journal, planner, two columns, margin
• Paper colours: cream, ivory, white, lemon, peach, pink, lavender, blue, mint, kraft, night
• Write text or draw with pen, pencil, fountain pen, brush, gel, fineliner and marker; eraser, lines, shapes, stars, arrows, check marks, images
• Multiple pages per notebook, undo/redo, zoom, reading mode
• Export a notebook to PDF; save and restore all notebooks with a backup file
• Interface in Azerbaijani, Turkish, English, Russian and Arabic

Privacy: your notes are stored only on your device. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app does not use the internet.
