Nibras Note privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026 · App version 1.0.0

1. Summary

Nibras Note is a notebook app for your phone. Everything you write and draw is stored only on your device. There is no account, no server, no ads and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

2. Information we do not collect

The developer does not collect or receive your name, e-mail, location, contacts, device identifiers, analytics data, or the content of your notes. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not request the internet permission.

3. Data stored on your device

Your notebooks (title, cover, paper style, text, pen strokes, images you insert) and settings (language, pen, default paper) are kept only in the app's private storage on your device.

4. Images and files

When you insert an image or import a backup, only the file you pick is read. Files are never uploaded.

5. Backup and export

"Save to file" and PDF export write a file only where you choose. If you share it with another app, that app's own privacy policy applies.

6. Deletion

Use Settings → Delete all notebooks, or uninstall the app, to remove all data. Because we hold nothing on any server, there is nothing for us to delete remotely.

7. Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date and publish the new version on this page.
